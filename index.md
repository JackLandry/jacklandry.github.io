I’m the founder and research lead at Civic Data Atlas, a platform providing comprehensive access to city council and neighborhood data. Previously, I was a lead researcher at the Jain Family Institute, where I worked on welfare policy and tax issues. Before that, I was a research professional at the [University of Chicago Harris School of Public Policy](https://harris.uchicago.edu/). I earned a B.A. in Economics and Political Science from [Rutgers University–New Brunswick](https://newbrunswick.rutgers.edu/) in 2018.
